[daisyUI Modal](https://daisyui.com/components/modal/) — native `<dialog>`. Open with `id.showModal()`, close with Esc, click outside, or buttons inside the modal. Order of children doesn't matter — they get placed correctly.

- **`Modal`** — Root `<dialog>`. Required `id`. Optional `placement` (`top` / `middle` / `bottom`), `alignment` (`start` / `end`), `responsive`. Backdrop + close-on-click-outside by default. **`noBackdrop`** hides the backdrop (click outside still closes). **`closeOnClickOutside={false}`** disables click-to-close. Combine both to get no backdrop and no click-to-close.
- **`ModalBox`** — Content container. Pass `class` for custom width.
- **`ModalActions`** — Footer row. Buttons inside auto-close the modal.
- **`ModalClose`** — Corner ✕ button. Override content via slot.

### Basic

Backdrop is built in — clicking outside closes the modal.

      <Modal id="modal_basic">
        <ModalBox>
          <h3 class="text-lg font-bold">Hello!</h3>
          <p class="py-4">Press Esc, click outside, or click Close.</p>
          <ModalActions>
